# Stickman Studio's own look

- 1920x1080, thick dark outlines with a slight wobble, flat fills, paper-coloured backgrounds, captions in the bottom 180 px.
- Nations and sides are hat kinds (`studio/engine/pen.py` HATS) plus a coat colour; people keep one look for the whole video.
- Faces: dot or wide eyes, small mouths; reactions are face changes, not extra drawings.
- Props are drawn in the same flat style; text on props uses the bold font for titles and the handwriting font for body lines.
- The mascot ("Sticky") is the only character that appears in every video.
