# Historical animation patterns

- **Maps**: dark map style, countries tinted on their names, cities as dots with labels, arrows with marching units for movement, a
  big year counter. One map idea per scene. `[seed]`
- **Crowds** stand for "the people" (cheering, rioting, queueing, migrating); a single named figure stands for "the leader". `[seed]`
- **Battles** are two blocks and a commander each, never a swarm; the *result* is shown by one flag falling and one commander reacting. `[seed]`
- **Politics** happens in rooms: a chamber with benches, a table with a treaty between two leaders, a podium with a flag. The document
  is always visible and readable. `[seed]`
- **Large numbers** become counters and rows of icons, then are compared to something familiar. `[seed]`
- **Serious events** (deaths, famine, massacres): dark palette, slow zoom, fades, plain wording, numbers within historians' ranges,
  no jokes, no gore. `[seed]`
- **Jokes** come from contrast and timing: an absurd detail drawn plainly, a one-line bubble landing on the key word, a reaction face. `[seed]`
- **Eras**: weapons, clothes and buildings match the year (`review.check_history`). `[seed]`
- **Places as full backgrounds**: construction sites, battlefields, courtrooms, farms, mines fill the frame and move; a small prop on
  an empty page is the failure case. `[user clip]`
